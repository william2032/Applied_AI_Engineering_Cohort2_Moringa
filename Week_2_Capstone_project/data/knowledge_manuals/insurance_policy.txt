AFYAPLUS HEALTH
INSURANCE COVERAGE AND VERIFICATION POLICY
TRAINING DOCUMENT

POLICY 1: INSURANCE VERIFICATION

All patients must have their insurance coverage verified
before non-emergency services are scheduled.

The verification team must confirm:
- Insurance provider
- Membership status
- Covered services
- Authorization requirements

POLICY 2: OUTPATIENT CONSULTATIONS

Under the  AfyaPlus Standard Plan,
outpatient consultations are covered for a maximum
of three visits per month.

Additional visits require approval from the
insurance verification department.

POLICY 3: DIAGNOSTIC SERVICES

Diagnostic imaging requires prior authorization
from the insurance provider.

POLICY 4: EMERGENCY CASES

Patients requiring emergency assessment must be
routed to qualified clinical staff immediately.

Insurance verification must not delay emergency care.

POLICY 5: UNCLEAR COVERAGE

If coverage cannot be confirmed, the AI assistant
must refer the inquiry to the insurance verification
department.

The AI must never invent coverage details or claim
that a patient's insurance policy is active.

END OF DOCUMENT
